Install the Field app

Record registration entries and plan tomorrow's kit movements — right from your phone, no browser tabs.

  • Works offline Enter counts without signal — data syncs automatically when you reconnect.
  • One tap to open Lives on your home screen. No browser, no typing URLs.
  • Yours alone This device is bound to your ward — nobody else can enter your kit's data.
Open in browser

To install on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari if you aren't already.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an up arrow) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. The app appears on your home screen.

To install on Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home Screen.
  4. Confirm. The app appears on your home screen.
